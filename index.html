Humanoid Civilization

Society (v2) Economy (v1)

Learning agents live whole lives in one small town. Every hour each of them decides what to do: sleep, walk to work, work a shift, take a lunch break at the canteen, buy groceries at the market, go home, raise children, make friends, steal or stop a thief. Nothing is scripted: the routines you see are what they learned, because their only reward is how good their life feels. Pick a training stage to watch how their days change as they learn.
